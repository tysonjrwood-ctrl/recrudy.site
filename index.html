The problem we lived with

Most recruitment software was built for the people who sell it, not the people who use it.

Admin eats the week

Fields nobody fills in, screens nobody needs, and the same details typed into three places. Hours that should go to clients and candidates.

Search eats the hours, and the budget

A search-platform seat, a talent-database subscription, then paid messages to someone already in your own database. Each tool adds time and cost, and none of them talk to each other.

Risk slips through the gaps

An expired check, a fee that doesn't match the terms, a guarantee nobody tracked, a candidate's CV pasted into an AI tool with their personal details still on it. Problems that cost a placement, a client, or your reputation.
