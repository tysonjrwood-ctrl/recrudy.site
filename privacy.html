Privacy Policy

How Recrudy handles personal information. Last updated 15 November 2026.

Recrudy Pty Ltd (ACN 703 668 697) ("Recrudy", "we", "us") makes recruitment software for agencies. We are an Australian company and follow the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. Where the GDPR or the New Zealand Privacy Act applies to someone, we respect the rights it gives them too.

1. Who this policy covers

  • Visitors to recrudy.com.au and people who contact us, join the pilot or book a demo.
  • Agencies and their users: the recruitment agencies that use Recrudy, and their staff.
  • Candidates and client contacts whose details an agency keeps in Recrudy. For this information, the agency decides how it is used and is responsible for it under privacy law. Recrudy holds and processes it on the agency's behalf, following the agency's instructions. If you are a candidate or client contact, the agency's own privacy policy also applies, and it is usually best to contact the agency first.

2. What we collect

  • From visitors: what you send us (your name, work email, agency name and message), and basic technical information about your visit, such as your browser, device, IP address and pages viewed.
  • From agencies: your agency's business details, and each user's name, work email, role and sign-in details (including passkeys or two-step sign-in), billing details, and records of how Recrudy is used, such as AI credit use.
  • On agencies' behalf: the candidate and client information an agency puts into Recrudy or receives through it. This can include contact details, CVs, work history, qualifications, compliance checks (such as licences or working-with-children checks), applications, notes, placements and emails.
  • Public business information: to help agencies map their market, Recrudy holds a directory of organisations (such as schools and law firms), built from public sources like their websites. This is information about organisations, for example names, addresses, logos and practice areas.

3. How we use it

  • To provide Recrudy, set up accounts, keep sign-ins secure and give support.
  • To bill agencies and manage plans and AI credit limits.
  • To reply to enquiries, run the pilot and arrange demos.
  • To keep Recrudy secure, prevent misuse, and fix and improve it.
  • To tell agency users about changes to Recrudy. You can opt out of marketing emails at any time.

We use candidate and client information only to provide Recrudy to the agency it belongs to. We never sell personal information, and we never use one agency's candidates or clients for another agency.

4. Artificial intelligence

  • Recrudy has optional AI features, such as Rudy, CV reading and formatting, and document writing. They use Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic.
  • Recrudy is built to remove personal details, such as a candidate's name and contact details, before a CV is sent to AI. Agency users check what has been removed, and are asked to tell us if anything gets through.
  • Only the information needed for the task is sent, and it is sent encrypted.
  • Anthropic processes it on our behalf under its commercial terms. It does not use it to train its models, and deletes it from its systems within 30 days, unless it must keep it longer by law or to enforce its usage policy.
  • Anthropic's services run outside Australia, including in the United States. We take reasonable steps to make sure the information is handled consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.
  • AI doesn't make decisions about candidates. Agencies and their clients make every hiring decision.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run Recrudy, under contracts that require them to protect it and use it only for us:

  • Laravel Cloud and Amazon Web Services, which host Recrudy and its files in Sydney;
  • Anthropic, for AI features (see section 4);
  • identity and compliance check providers, such as GBG, when an agency asks a candidate to verify their identity and the candidate agrees;
  • email, sign-in and payment providers, such as Microsoft, Google and our payment processor;
  • job boards an agency chooses to advertise on, which receive the advert and send applications back.

We may also share information where the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

6. Storage and security

  • Recrudy's data is stored in Australia, on Amazon Web Services in Sydney. It is encrypted in transit and at rest, and backed up on a schedule.
  • Each agency's data is kept separate. Users see only what their agency allows them to see.
  • Sign-in is protected by passwords, passkeys and two-step sign-in.
  • If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the agencies affected and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and the people involved, under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as the law requires. When an agency closes its account, it can export its data for 30 days. After that, we delete it, except where we must keep records by law (for example, tax records).

8. Your rights

  • You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and to correct it.
  • Where the law gives you the right, you can also ask us to delete it, limit how we use it, or give it to you in a portable form.
  • If your information is held by an agency in Recrudy, we will pass your request to that agency and help it respond.
  • We don't charge for requests, unless one is clearly unfounded or excessive. We'll confirm who you are before we share anything.

9. Cookies

This website and Recrudy use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your settings and keep the service secure. We may also count visits to understand how the website is used, without identifying you. You can turn off cookies in your browser, but parts of Recrudy may not work without them.

10. Questions and complaints

For any question, request or complaint about privacy, please contact our Data Protection Officer at dataprotection@recrudy.com.au. We aim to acknowledge you within two business days and respond in writing within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner on 1300 363 992 or at www.oaic.gov.au.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, and we'll tell agencies about important changes.
